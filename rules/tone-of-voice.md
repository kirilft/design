# Tone of voice

- Define the tone before writing copy: how we present ourselves and how we address the user (“we” or “I”, formal or informal, professional or casual). Keep it the same everywhere.
- Describe the tone in 3 words (for example friendly, direct, playful). Write 2–3 example sentences that show it. Use them as the reference for all copy.
- Short words over long words. Short sentences over long sentences. Short paragraphs over long text.
- Users read short paragraphs. They skip long text.
